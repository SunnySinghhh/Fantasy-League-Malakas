League Constitution
The governing document, for when the fine print isn't enough

Article I

Purpose

This constitution governs Malaka's United, a 12-team fantasy football league. Where this document and the League Rules page disagree, this constitution takes precedence — Rules covers this season's specifics (dates, payouts, scoring); this document covers how the league governs itself season over season.

Article II

Governance

Section 1 — Commissioner

The Commissioner administers the league day to day: runs the draft lottery, processes payouts, settles disputes not covered by these rules, and keeps the site up to date. The Commissioner does not have unilateral authority to change scoring, roster settings, or payout structure mid-season without a league vote.

Section 2 — Decisions

Day-to-day rulings (trade fairness, waiver disputes, lineup mistakes) are decided by the Commissioner. Anything that changes the rules for future seasons requires a league vote under the Amendment process below.

Article III

Membership

Section 1 — Returning Members

All 12 teams are expected to return each season unless a member opts out before the following year's draft. Buy-in must be paid before the draft to retain a roster spot.

Section 2 — Replacement

If a team owner leaves the league, the Commissioner finds a replacement before the next draft. A replacement owner inherits the outgoing owner's roster and draft slot as-is.

Article IV

Amendments

How this document — or the League Rules it governs — actually gets changed.

  1. Propose. Any owner can propose a rule change at any point in the offseason.
  2. Discuss. The proposal is put in front of the full league for discussion.
  3. Vote. A majority of owners must approve the change for it to take effect.
  4. Record. Approved changes are logged in the Change Log below and take effect the following season, unless the league votes to apply it immediately.
Article V

Change Log

Every amendment made to league governance, scoring, or structure, in order.
