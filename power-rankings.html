The Model

Power Rankings

Not just the standings — a blended score of record, scoring, recent form, and margin, recomputed for every week of league history.

How the Power Score works

Each team gets a 0–100 Power Score built from four ingredients, each measured against the rest of the league that week:

  • Win percentage (35%) — record so far, ties counted as half a win.
  • Season scoring (25%) — average points per game all season.
  • Recent form (25%) — average points per game over the last 3 weeks, so hot and cold streaks matter.
  • Point margin (15%) — average scoring margin, win or lose.

Every ingredient is measured in standard deviations from that week's league average, so it self-balances: in Week 1 records are all 1–0 or 0–1 and barely separate anyone, so scoring and margin naturally carry more weight — record only takes over once there's a real sample to judge it by. 50 is a league-average team; the score just reflects that specific week's field. Regular season only — playoff seeding upsets don't rewrite the story. Each season is scored entirely on its own: a 2022 Week 3 team is measured only against its own 12 opponents that year, never against another season's field, so no year can shift another year's numbers.
